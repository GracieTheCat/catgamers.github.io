HELLO! WELCOME TO CATGAMERS!

We are glad to have you as a part of our community. We are a new gaming group that wants gaming to be a safe environment for others who are new or experienced.

OUR MISSION

Our mission in CatGamers is to provide a PG-13 environment for new and experienced gamers online. We like to help gamers enjoy games with others and help build each other up. We have 4 pillars of code that we follow:

TEAMWORK

Working together is an important thing in our community. Teamwork does wonders to a person when helped, so it is very encouraged in this community that you help out your fellow gamers, especially when they are struggling.

SPORTSMANSHIP

In games it could get frustrating, but one of our values is being a good sport about it. You don’t have to like someone to be respectful to them, and so it is very encouraged that you use gamer etiquette in spite of your situation.

CIVILITY

Outside of the game, we still encourage you to be civil about your actions and words. Please be respectful to fellow members and staff in CatGamers, and it is very encouraged to do nice things for fellow members.

CREATIVITY

We want our members to be creative in our community, and so we encourage you to draw, write, and do other mediums of work and share it with the community as long as it is appropriate. We are against Artificial Intelligence in the Creative community and if we catch you using AI for your final product, we will take it down. Any use of AI should be disclaimed.
